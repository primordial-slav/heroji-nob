# Surface brief: whole site (redesign)

Scope: all routes (home, unit page, record popup, Izvori). O nama and Kontakt were removed by the owner. Visitor mode: Operate (find one person), with Read on the text pages.
Audience: families and descendants, often on phones. Job: type a name, find the person, see the book line.
Constraints: keep page structure, unit photos, theme toggle, Partisan red. No stats, no formation-date ordering. Not SaaS, not academic.
Build path: code-led (no image generation, no engine; rules applied from craft-floor.md and operate.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a place to find one person. It refuses the dashboard (stat tiles, card grids, hover-lift tiles) and the wartime costume (stars, fake paper, glowing caps).

OWN-WORLD: Red cloth and printed rosters. A solid flag-red band carries the name, nav and, on the home page, the search itself. Below it, plain neutral ground, black ink, 1px rules, square corners, no shadows except the popup. Names in PT Serif as the book prints them (surname first, surname bold); everything else in Golos Text. Unit photos shown as real photographs, never cropped to tiny badges.

STORY: A relative types a surname, sees matching lines that read like the book, opens one, and sees the scanned line highlighted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the red band holds the title, one sentence and a large white search field (the primary action), with example searches under it. Directly below, the list of units as photo-and-name rows, two per row on wide screens. When searching, results replace the unit list in place.

FORM: Code-led rebuild of the existing structure; own direction (no concept-seed roll, engine not installed).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance


## Outcome (2026-09-30)
After a quieter scale-down and a round of five variants (Toplina, Fotografije, Ljudi, Mirno, Knjiga), the owner chose "Fotografije": one brigade photograph multiplied into the red band behind the search, black-and-white photo cards, a full-width photograph on unit pages and a photo strip above each record. The variants are archived in .impeccable/variants-snapshot-2026-09-30.zip. DESIGN.md records the shipped system.
