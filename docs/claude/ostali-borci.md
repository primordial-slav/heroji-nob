# Borci drugih jedinica (Ostali borci, 122) and the census's parts (131-138)

Part of the project's notes: `CLAUDE.md` is the short core, loaded in every session, and this file holds one topic. It was moved here from `CLAUDE.md` as it stood on 2026-11-08; new notes on this topic go here.

**Ostali borci (code 122, `website/public/ostali-borci.json`).** On the site it is called "Borci drugih jedinica" (Slovene *Borci drugih enot*, Macedonian *Борци од други единици*, English *Fighters of other units*; the user's choice of 2026-10-28), its census parts "Borci drugih jedinica – Hrvatska" and so on; its id, address, files and the name in these notes stay "ostali-borci". Every soldier of a book across units has a place on the site: the entries no record of a unit took become records of their own, made by `data-extraction/build_ostali_borci.py` (62,060 records on 2026-10-21). One man printed in several of these books is one record: entries of one name from different books that agree (the father and one of birth year, birthplace, year of death; or birth year and birthplace; two hero books on the father or the birth year) and disagree on nothing are merged, an entry with a scan on the site first, then the longest, the others in `other_sources` (plain merged entries, no `ref_book`), the father the lead's, else (none or an initial) the first entry's that gives him in full, read as its book prints him (a census father beside the lead's initial: "Zec B. Drago", Bojan); an entry of the same man as an entry a record took stays out (878). The year of death is no clash where the father, the exact birth year and the village agree, or they and the place of death with the years one apart (the books often differ there, 1941 against 1944 an OCR slip as often as not). The Spomenica crnogorskim antifašistima prints a man under each municipality he belongs to (`PRINTS_TWICE`): two of its entries of one name are one man when the father, birth year, year of death and the place of death or village agree, or when they stand under two municipalities with the birth years one apart and the father in full (not one letter off; and not where they fit two such entries of another book, one each); the census's two entries of one name never are. Where a book prints two men of one name, another book's entry joins the one it agrees with best, a fit that needs the years of death to differ last, and a hero's two hero-book entries go together. `ONE_MAN` holds pairs checked by hand that an OCR slip in the father hides ("Recov" for Redžov), or in the surname (the census's "Usović" for Ćosović Veljko, whose Spomenica entry read so too until its surnames were read by the list's order on 2026-11-03, and the record kept the census's father from an entry it no longer held), and `TWO_MEN` pairs checked to be two men that the rules would join (a narodni heroj and a list that names no father, of one name and birth year: Nikolić Živojin-Brka and Han Pijesak's Žoja). Record IDs are kept in `data-extraction/ostali_borci_ids.json` (entry ID -> record ID): a man keeps his ID however his entries change, a new man gets the next free one from 0122900001 (the first records took 0122 + the book's two digits + the entry's number), so a book may have any number of entries, but a book's entry numbers must stay put. Books that give little more than the name (Gončin's and Tmušić's Srem) merge on a unit or the place of death together with the year of death or each other (`units`, `death_place`, `death_years`; the same span of years counts, and a brigade not on the site is compared by its number and name, `unit_key`: "Trideset prva srpska udarna brigada" and "Trideset prve srpske narodnooslobodilačke brigade" are "31 srpsk"). Their texts hold no bio ("Deseta krajiška udarna brigada, Đeletovci: Dušan Ačić."), so the field extractor reads only a duty from them (`NAME_LISTS`) and the builder sets the death from the entries' fields (poginuo, the village, the day); their PDFs are in `entry_boxes.INLINE_ENTRIES`, so the records keep the parser's box around the name. The file is not `*soldiers.json` on purpose: the parsers' corpora glob that, and its OCR leftovers must not teach them spellings. Trebinjci's possessive fathers ("Mitrov", "Lučin") get their nominative from the site's given names (Mitar, Luka), the possessive books' genitives too ("Jova" is Jovo's, not a daughter's possessive), and a genitive father ("Miloša", "Franca") the nominative `convert_fathers_genitive.py` would choose for a unit's record (the units get it as corrections, 122 can't), by the book's own given names first (the Spomenica's "Mila" is Milo, Lika's Mile), then the site's (`from_genitive`; the normalizer's own dictionary leaves most as printed), and fathers a book prints in the nominative, as the census does, go to `fathers_name` and not `middle_name` (`NOMINATIVE_FATHERS`: the Tuzla memorial book); `NAME_FIXES` holds names a parser read wrong, `GIVEN_FIXES` given names an OCR slip spoiled ("Miilan", "Petair"; the text keeps them as printed), and an entry its book marks `unnamed` gets no record; one the book names by a single name, a surname or a given name alone (Romano's "Ašer (prezime nepoznato)", Oborovo's "Ivica ...", Gončin's "Pančeri"), does (the user's decision of 2026-10-14), and never joins another book's entry by that name alone. `match_references.py` never matches an entry to unit 122, and `find_source_duplicates.py --across` never links to it. After a book's references are written (or a new unit takes some of these men), rebuild it, in this order (on 2026-10-27 it gave back the committed files, their records, IDs, fathers and census parts, but for 510 fields of 505 records of 122 that rules written after the fields were first filled read otherwise, and 6 census places of death, "Žumberk" now Žumberak; run again, it changes nothing. After that day's rows of the card and identity pass it read 26 birthplaces worse ("u Užicu" Užica, "u Kaknju" Kaknja), since the known places' vote lacked endings; with them (2026-10-28, see "Structured fields") the rebuild was taken: 41 junk places of death gone ("Mesto pogibije nepoznato", "Student iz Našica"), 65 gained, the cases put right (Kladanj, Tešanj, Pljevlja, Krušik), and the Zagorje's Jablanovec (three Hrvatsko zagorje men) and one Škofja Loka kept by `case_forms.json`'s `births`, since the table's "Jablanovcu" is Lika's Jablanovac): (1) `python data-extraction/build_ostali_borci.py --write`, which keeps each 122 record's father as the file it replaces put him in the nominative while the father is printed so (`chosen_fathers`: the choice reads the units' given names and how often a genitive still stands unconverted in them, which change as the units' own fathers get corrections; blank a `fathers_name` in the file to have it chosen again; the printed form is compared as the normalizer cleans it, so the choice holds when the builder runs twice before step (2): on 2026-11-19 a second run read its own file's "Đ." against the cleaned "Đ", dropped Ćosović Veljko's Đorđije, and the normalizer wrote the initial in its place; an initial is never kept as a choice; a possessive no name reads from, one the scan spoiled ("Milivojsv", "Sgankov"), gives way to an entry whose father its book's rule reads, another book's "Milivoja" or the census's nominative, while a book's own nominative and a rare name the other books read no better stay: 69 fathers on that day, Drašković Ilija's "M" among them); (2) `python scripts/normalize_all_json.py --brigade 122 131 132 133 134 135 136 137 138 --apply` (the builder writes `full_name` empty); (3) `python scripts/extract_structured_fields.py --brigade 122 --apply` (122 has no corrections, so `apply_corrections.py` reads no field for it; the census parts' fields are the builder's, from the census's columns); (4) `python scripts/apply_corrections.py --apply --brigade 122 131 132 133 134 135 136 137 138` (or without `--brigade`, every unit): the women's nicknames, the places in the nominative and the places of death tidied (left out for 131-138, their places of death stay in a case: "Pločama", "Beogradu"), the entry boxes, and the life events from the final records; After a change to the field reader, run the chain from step (1), never steps (3)-(4) alone on the file as it stands: the reader fills only empty fields, so a value an older reading stored stays until the builder writes the records anew, and the next full run then puts the record's own entry first (2026-11-20: the rebuild of the "ili" places ran steps (3)-(4) alone and kept 9 dates and 5 places that the Vrebac and Zavođe list had filled where the record's own entry, readable since, says otherwise, Narančić Milanka Nikola's "20.12.1944" against his own "oktobra 1944"; 05's chain from step (1) gave the own entry first and the list's date after "ili", which looked like a swap). The builder and `apply_corrections.py` write through `scripts/safe_write.py` since that night: a MemoryError half-way through a write had emptied `ostali-borci-vojvodina.json`. The places in the nominative need the case forms of these files kept in `data-extraction/places/ostali_cases.json` (see "Resolved places"). Compare the rebuilt files with the committed ones field by field before committing (the local `quality/field_diff.py`); a difference is a rule that reads an entry otherwise than when the field was first filled. The field extractor learns its vocabulary from the units only, not from unit 122. On the home page its card stands last, under "Bez jedinice" (`UnitsByYear`). A reference correction must never point at a 0122 record: the record goes away when its man finds his unit. The 1964 census's men whom only the census names are Ostali borci too, in eight parts of their own, one a volume (codes 131-138, `CENSUS_VOLUMES` and `OSTALI_CODES` in `scripts/name_utils.py`; files `ostali-borci-<area>.json`; on the home page with 122 under "Bez jedinice", not as cards but as one panel beside 122's card, each part's region and count, which `UnitsByYear` makes of every unit whose id starts `ostali-borci-`): a unit of 160,000 would pass Vercel's 19 MB for one search part and GitHub's 100 MB for one file. Such a record's ID is his census entry's number under his part's code (9021004235 -> 0132004235), outside the registry; a census man another book also names is a record of 122. Two census entries are never merged (each volume lists its dead once, and one name, father and year in two republics are two men). The census's columns are the record's fields (birthplace, nationality, how and when he died, where), and the field extractor doesn't read its text. A 122 record that joins a column book or the census with a book that prints a biography takes its kind and date of death from the columns first, marked `from_columns`, and the field reader then puts the man's own biography first for those two (2026-10-28, from the 05 session's sample: "Strijeljana od četnika 1942." is streljan, not the Spomenica's "poginuo"; "poginuo 9. juna 1943." the day, not the column's year), a date only where the biography's is at least as exact (Ludbreg's battle day stays). Since 2026-11-20 the place of death is marked too (the census's "Bosiljevo slove" had stood over Sekulić Mane Miloš's own "u Žumberku 10. 9. 1944.", set right by correction 316872 until then), and the reader puts the place his own entries give first (`own_place_first`) unless it says less: a region or a land where the column names a place ("Bosna" for Tuzla, "Lika" for Plješevica, "Sremski front" for Irig; a mountain or a battle is often closer than the census's municipality, "Petrova gora" for Vojnić, and is taken), a reading the scan garbled (`SCAN_GARBLE_RE`, mostly the Lovćenski odred's Cyrillic read as Latin: "u Don>oj Trepči", "Mapra . u Peći", "Ex ., Mateševo"), or a place that goes with a death of another year than the date the record keeps; and of two forms of one place (a word in common) his entries' only where it says more and the map knows it ("Jadovnik, Sjenica" for Sjenica) or mends a column the map can't place or the census cut ("Preboj" Prijeboj, "Donja kupčin" Donja Kupčina, "Bos krupa"), else the census's, in its one form and on the map ("Šumane, Pljevlja", not "Pljevlja"; "Krtoli", not "u Krtolima"). The census's place then shows after "ili" where the two lie apart (`record_sources.variants`: "Zuleševica ili 1944, Gospić"); where the site can't place one of them it shows only in the census's reference. The dry count over the 6,770 records so marked found the reader's place differing from the column's in about 3,100; a first rebuild changed 2,266 places of death, 933 of them to a place the site can't put on the map, mostly a village or a phrase the books print ("na Lučkim Kolibama", "Batinska skela"). The region test reads a region's words in their cases ("u Srijemu", "na sremskom frontu", "u Crnoj Gori"). The rebuild with both then changed 2,225 places of death against the day before (338 another place, apart; 336 near or inside it; 860 a place the site can't put on the map; 691 another form of the same place) and 388 records' versions after "ili" (23 gained, 6 lost); 6,522 records carry the place in `from_columns`. In the same run `read_all` takes the day another of his books gives in the year (and month) his first entry gives alone (`finer_same_year`, the lead's Bogdanović Stevana Dušan: "u Trnovcu kod Gospića 1943." beside 9048000003's "16. juna 1943."; a date in the same year is no other version): 105 dates; and "na osiguranju" ends a place (`TAIL_ANYWHERE_RE`: "na Ivčević Kosi kod Perušića na osiguranju OK Perušić"; the capital of "Kosi" is the Karlovac book's own text, pp. 21, 34, 35, 46).
