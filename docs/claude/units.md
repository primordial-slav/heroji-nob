## Formation dates, kind and republic

**Formation dates**: the home page groups units by the year they were formed, from `website/app/data/formation.ts` (`date` as `YYYY-MM-DD`, or `YYYY-MM`/`YYYY`, plus the source). A new unit needs an entry there, from its book or another source you checked; without one it is listed last, under "Ostale jedinice".

**Kind and republic**: the Jedinice page (/jedinice) filters by kind (brigade, divizije, odredi, ostale) and by the republic a unit came from (Bosna i Hercegovina, Crna Gora, Hrvatska, Makedonija, Slovenija, Srbija with Vojvodina and Kosovo), from `website/app/data/unitKinds.ts`, set by hand: the NOVJ tables' sections where they are right (they put the Montenegrin brigades under Bosnia), the formation place and the unit's book otherwise; a unit of men from two (Prva proleterska, the sandžačka) has both, a unit of the whole army (the proletarian divisions, the Vrhovni štab, the air units) none. A new unit needs a line there; without one its kind comes from its id and it has no republic. The filters and the year go in the address (`?godina=1943&vrsta=brigada&republika=hrvatska`).
