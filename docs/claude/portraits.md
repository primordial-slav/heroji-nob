no drawings, graves, busts or execution photos; the unit's book wins). Most units' books on znaci.org have now been searched this way; photos whose caption names the soldier but agrees on nothing more were left out. The books are cached in `data-extraction/.cache/znaci_photos/books/`, the Commons files as 500-px thumbnails in `.cache/commons/` (Wikimedia rate-limits scripts that pull originals). A click on the print opens the photo over the page (`components/PhotoLightbox.tsx`), at most twice its size: from `<soldier id>-v.jpg` (up to 900 px high) where the source is at least half again as large as the print. The Galerija page (`app/views/GalleryPage.tsx`, at /galerija) shows every portrait, small and in a new random order on each visit; a click opens the record. A man with a portrait under several records (one in each unit or book that prints him: a narodni heroj's photo goes to each) is shown once: his other index entries carry `same`, the id of the record shown (the first by `units.ts` order), which `mark_same` in the script sets from the same print, records linked across units, and a hand-checked list (`SAME`, for unlinked records with photos of their own); `--same` re-marks the index without rendering anything. On screens wide enough to have margins, the home page has wartime portraits drifting slowly up both sides (`components/PortraitRails.tsx`). These come only from `data/wartimePortraits.ts`: photos picked by eye from contact sheets (uniforms, partisan caps, young faces in period prints), plus every soldier in the index who died in the war, unless the print is too washed out to show a face. Most of the Tuzlanski book's portraits were taken decades after the war and stay out. A new portrait goes on that list only if it is a wartime photo, and once per person: a man printed in several units' books has his photo under each record, and only one goes on the list. Each print there, and the print or grey stand-in in the record dialog and on the Spomen-kartica (on a white mount, in the light theme's inks, printed), wears a thin embroidered frame (`lib/vez.ts`; the user's design of 2026-10-28): 136 frames in 46 sets (`scripts/make_vez_frames.py` writes `public/vez/<n>.svg`, one mask per thread over its ink, and `app/data/vez.json`), a set for each part of a land (Lika, Kordun, Banija, Srem, Banat, Šumadija, Sandžak, Herzegovina, Cazinska krajina and the rest, each from that part's own needlework and all its peoples', none named), one for each minority or foreign nationality a record prints (Jews, Albanians, Slovaks, Czechs, Rusyns, Ukrainians, Russians, Turks, Roma; Italians, Germans and Austrians, Hungarians, of whom the rails show one face at most), and a neutral set for a soldier whose birthplace says nothing. `scripts/build_vez.py` gives each soldier his set by his nationality, else by the part his birthplace lies in (its GeoNames place or the town it names, the nearest of each part's towns), and writes the set numbers only, never a region: `website/data/vez-sets.json`, which the record dialog gets with its place files (`/data/places/of/<shard>.json`, `v`), and `app/data/railVez.json` for the rails; the frame within the set comes from his name, so he wears the same one on the rails and in his record. Run it after `build_relations.py`, and after `make_vez_frames.py` changed the sets. The rails also show every face once whatever the list holds: one picture once (a Commons file or a znaci gallery photo, else the file), one person once (an index entry's `same`, or two ids that lead to one record linked across units). Search results never show photos. A soldier without one gets an anonymous grey outline of a partisan in a cap with its red star (`data/silhouettes.ts`, made from period portraits by `scripts/make_standin_silhouettes.py`; the šajkača goes to some Serbs of the units from Serbia and Bosnia, `lib/standIn.ts`).

**Heroes in Borci drugih jedinica (2026-11-22).** No record of Borci drugih jedinica (122) had a portrait, though 1,087 of them are narodni heroji: the 1982 hero book's portraits had only gone to unit records holding its entry as a reference. `HEROES_1982_ENTRIES` in `scripts/extract_book_portraits.py` holds 354 more of that book's portraits, keyed by the book's entry, not a record: `holders()` gives each to the record that holds the entry at each run (a unit record's reference, else the 122 record `data-extraction/ostali_borci_ids.json` names), so the photo follows a man who later finds his unit. A row of COMMONS may be keyed by an entry the same way (a `9...` id). The placing rule was rebuilt in the session's scratchpad (`heroes82.py`, not kept): the photo layer (white, the photos only) cut into photos, two pieces of one joined and a page number trimmed off; each entry's heading matched on its own page (the book's page + 4); a photo taken only between the entry's heading and the very next heading on the page, which must be the next entry's, and only where the entry holds no other photo. Every row of `HEROES_1982` that both place (233) agrees, and each of the 354 was looked at against its heading (left out: Filipović Stjepan at the gallows, Trbović Rajko's bust, Ristić Sofija's faded photo, Ostojić Jakov's too dark to show a face). Arsenić Borko, the 12. dalmatinska's commander, has the hero book's photo on his staff-list record too: the brigade's book gives his birth as the hero's entry, and its own photo of him is washed out.

**Captions read again (2026-11-22).** The photos of every cached book were matched again against the records of the day (staff lists, second books and names mended since October), and three kinds were taken, each photo looked at: portraits in pairs captioned "(levo)", "(desno)", "(v sredini)" (Strle's *Tomšičeva brigada*, knj. 2-4: 70 men, 86 records; the photo on that side, cut to it where the scan holds a sliver of the next one; a name the site has once, or a record that gives the same partisan name), a portrait over a woman's biography in *Žene Srbije u NOB* (00003/614) and *Žene Hrvatske u NOB* (35 women: her given name, surname and birth year), and 20 single portraits whose caption names the man and agrees with his record on a duty, a nickname, a birth or a death. `UNIT_BOOKS` rows may now be keyed by a book's entry too (a man of Borci drugih jedinica), resolved by `holders()`. Left out: group photos whose caption gives no one's place, a gravestone, a photo of two portraits in one, and a man whose record names another duty in another unit (Trivić Vukan).

**Wikipedia's partisans (2026-11-22).** The categories of Yugoslav partisans, holders of the spomenica 1941, JNA generals and narodni heroji on the sr, sh, hr, bs, sl, mk and en Wikipedia (about 5,500 people with Wikidata's), each article's free lead photo and Wikidata's birth and death, were matched to the records without a portrait: the same birth year and the same birthplace or death (each place by all its names in Wikidata), never a record that fell in the war against a man who lived after it, and the name fitting one person on the site; where only the year and a big town agree, the name must be the only one of its kind on the site, or the record agrees on more (a nickname, the exact day, a village). Each photo was looked at as the 3:4 print: drawings, busts, statues, graves, reliefs, group photos, faces too small or covered, and files that state no license left out; Franjo Tuđman's only free photo is of 1995, as president, and was left out. 202 people, 226 records. A file kept on a local Wikipedia (18) is named `host|file` in `COMMONS` and credited "Wikipedia". Wikimedia answered 429 to quick requests: the copies were fetched one every 12 seconds, with a User-Agent naming the site.
