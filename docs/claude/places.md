# Places: the places index and resolved places

Part of the project's notes: `CLAUDE.md` is the short core, loaded in every session, and this file holds one topic. It was moved here from `CLAUDE.md` as it stood on 2026-11-08; new notes on this topic go here.

**Places index** (`website/public/relations/places.json`) groups soldiers of all units by birthplace (village + municipality, folded spellings) for the record popup's "Saborci i zemljaci" tree; its unit and same-day branches are worked out in the browser from `unit_detail` and `death_date`. The popup doesn't load the whole index: `website/scripts/prepare-data.ts` cuts it into small files (`app/lib/placeFiles.ts`), `/data/places/of/<first 7 digits of the id>.json` (which chunk each soldier's place is in) and `/data/places/chunk/<n>.json` (~24 KB of places each), so a record loads its own place alone. It is not part of `apply_corrections.py`: after a run that adds records or changes birth places, rebuild it with `python scripts/build_relations.py --write` (dry run without `--write` prints coverage and the rules' samples), then `python scripts/build_vez.py --write` (each soldier's set of embroidered frames, see "Soldier Portraits"). A village printed without its municipality is linked only when the unit's own records, or the corpus, give it one municipality. Where a birthplace resolved to one place (below), that place is its key: its spellings and cases join ("Borovita, Glina" and "Borovitoj, Glina"; the Čačanski odred's "Zablaću" with the census's "Zablaće, Mrčajevci"), and two villages of one name stay apart ("Petrinja, Bosanski Novi" is not Petrinja; "Zagorje, Trbovlje" is Zagorje ob Savi); a printed key whose resolved records name one place, or one nearly all (85%, 10 records), takes its unresolved records along. The municipality shown is the one its records print most, none where most print none.

**Resolved places** (`data-extraction/places/resolved.json`, 2026-10-21): each unit's birthplaces and places of death resolved to one place of GeoNames (credited on the Izvori page, CC BY 4.0), only where something narrows the name to one: the only place of the name; the printed municipality (the candidate near its seat of administration, PPLA*, within 30 km, 15 km where GeoNames puts it in another of today's municipalities in Serbia, Montenegro or Kosovo, 50 km in the same; an island, mountain or valley printed in its place counts: "Pavlju, Brač"); the unit's own region (its resolved places within 25 km, three times the next; not for Ostali borci and the census parts, whose men come from everywhere); a town far bigger than the rest (never past a seat of administration: GeoNames gives the Serbian Priboj 3 people), unless the unit's region says a village of it; a well-known mountain, river or region as a place of death, in any case ("na Drini", "Romaniji"). A village no municipality confirms that lies far from everything else of its unit is left out ("Gornje Sušice" of the Tomšičeva is not the one in Kosovo), as are names of several places nothing decides, names GeoNames lacks, regions, Montenegro's tribes (Piva, Pješivci, Crmnica), foreign places (a "Gorica" alone may be Gorizia), and "Crna Gora, Žabljak" is Žabljak. Where a settlement carries the printed name as its own, a place that has it only as another name, old, foreign or a variant, is not meant: "Gradac" is the villages of that name, never Čačak, which was called so before 1409 (the user's correction of 2026-10-21; so Karanovac is not Kraljevo, Osek not Osijek, Kotor not Kotoriba); a short form of a place's own name stays a candidate ("Petrovac" of Bosanski Petrovac), Kosovo's Serbian names count as its own (GeoNames gives them as other names of Pejë and the rest), and a name no settlement carries goes to the place that has it ("Beograd": GeoNames names Belgrade in English). 79% of birthplaces and 58% of places of death resolve. The file is written by our places tool in `quality/` (local, not in git: `python quality/places/gazetteer.py --site`, ~2 min); run it after a batch of new records, then `apply_corrections.py --apply` and `build_relations.py --write`. `scripts/resolved_places.py` reads it: `place_id()` for the places index, and `apply()`, which `apply_corrections.py` runs on every unit, puts a field whose village resolved through a case in the nominative, spelled as the place is named ("Užicu" Užice, "Bosanskom Petrovcu" Bosanski Petrovac, "Tugarima, Omiš" Tugari, Omiš): a form that can only be a case anywhere, one that could be a nominative too ("Kuli" of Kula, but "Radenkovići") only where the entry prints a preposition before it ("u Kuli"); never after a preposition the field keeps ("kod Bihaća"), never a census village's name, never a field corrections set. The entries' text stays as printed. The gazetteer keys a field as the files hold it when it runs, so once `apply()` has put "Podgrađu, Omiš" in the nominative its next run keys "Podgrađe, Omiš" alone (on 2026-10-24 the 414 case forms of 122's birthplaces went so); Ostali borci and the census parts, which the builder writes anew, read the case again, so their case forms are kept in `data-extraction/places/ostali_cases.json` (every one `resolved.json` has keyed for those files, 952 gathered from its history on 2026-10-27; `remember()`, which `apply_corrections.py --apply` runs first, adds new ones), read where `resolved.json` has no key for the field. Where GeoNames settles nothing (a name of several places, or of none it knows), a place stored in a case still goes into the nominative from `data-extraction/places/case_forms.json` (2026-10-24), the table our local `quality/places/case_forms.py` writes once each form is read against its entries: "na Sutjesci" is Sutjeska, "u s. Bazje, Šid" Bazje, Šid, "u Tešnju" Tešanj, "u Zarkovu" Žarkovo. Only after "u", "na", "v", "s." or "selu" ("kod Bihaća", "pri Ribnici" stay); after "s." or "selu" the village is mostly printed in the nominative already, so only the word goes ("u s. Amajlije"); a bare form only where the table lists it as plainly a case (the census's "Pločama", "Vlasenici", Ostali borci's birthplace "Šapcu"; the census's "Rajinci", "Perci", "Pavlešenci" are villages' own names); 61 forms read and left as printed ("Galovcu": Galovac or Galovec; the dashboard no longer counts them). `apply()` runs it (`case_nominative`) where the resolved places have nothing: 1,311 places of death and 59 birthplaces on that day, 35 of the deaths set by older corrections and mended there (274377-274411). A form of the table stored without its preposition changes where the record's entry prints one before it (2026-10-25: the birthplaces read after "roden", "Ripnju, Beograd" of "rođen 1923. u Ripnju - Beograd", Crna Vlast, D. Štrpci: 264 birthplaces and 37 places of death); 27 such forms read and left as printed (Sali and Katuni are nominatives). The older birthplaces stored in a case were read on 2026-10-26 (the local `quality/places/birth_cases/`): 3,438 forms in 6,944 records whose entry prints "u" before the form, each form's nominatives by grammar weighed by the record's other entries and headings ("Borci poginuli u NOB, Dabar:"), GeoNames near the printed municipality, the heading's village or the unit's own region (the 1. kosovsko-metohijska's "u Peći" is Peć), and the census's villages of the općina, then read one by one where that left a doubt: 1,588 forms went into the table ("Dabru" Dabar, "L. Lešću" L. Lešće, "Kusatku" Kusadak, "Brocu" Brodac, "Novoj Varoši" Nova Varoš, "Docu Donjem" Dolac Donji, the 19. birčanska scan's "Rajiđima" Rajići), a form that is a case in one place and a village's own name in another went in record by record (`births`, soldier_id -> [the field as read, the new field], 335: "Marini, Trogir" is Marina, "u selu Marini, Prijedor" stays; the Zagorje's "Grgurovcu" is Grgurovec, Lebane's Grgurovac) (a record's entry holds while its field is as read or the same word in another case, `same_reading`: since the vote of 2026-10-28 reads "u Jarku" as Jarak itself, the table's Jarek wins; 2026-10-28 also Mursko Središće, which the vote made "Središče"), and 1,756 were left as printed: villages' own names after "u selu" (Laminci, Šahovići), forms of two readings ("Markoviću": Marković or Markovići) and the scans' garbles. A form that can also be a village's own name (`direct`, 182: "Peći", "Brezovici") changes only right after "u", never after "u selu" (the village Peći of Bosansko Grahovo). 3,877 birthplaces and 489 places of death on that day; a rewrite of the table (`case_forms.py --write`) takes the reading over its own earlier proposals.

**The census's cut place names** (2026-11-20). The 1964 census prints its place of death in a column cut at 15 letters: 15,214 of its places are exactly 15 long, many cut in a word ("Ilirska bistric", "Sremska mitrovi"), others whole names that happen to be so long ("Bosanska Dubica", "Generalski Stol"). The census parts showed them as printed, and since `record_sources.py` takes the census's place of death in as a version, unit cards did too ("10.09.1944, Žumberak ili 1944, Bosiljevo slove", 0122907839). `scripts/make_census_forms.py`, which writes the census place table (`data-extraction/places/census_forms.json`, read by `standardize_fields.place_form` for the census parts and for `record_sources`), now gives a cut name its whole form as a form only, the entry's text staying as printed. A whole name (`Places.cut`) takes the one known place it begins: a place GeoNames resolved (`resolved.json`) or one the units print 30 times ("Ilirska Bistrica", "Bosansko Grahovo", "Petrovac na Mlavi", "Radeče pri Zidanem mostu"), else one they print 3 times ("Podravski Kloštar"). A place under a twentieth of it is a misreading beside it ("Sremska Mitroviia"), anything more is another place, and the value stays ("Hrvatski karlov": Karlovac 4, Karlovec 1; "Cerovac tušilov"). A value ends where a known place ends and stays ("Grubišino Polje", "Zemun Sajmište"), and so does a case the site prints as a place ("Savinjski dolini"). An area after a village, joined by a hyphen or comma ("Duga resa-karlo"), keeps the five-times rule the table had since 11-03 (Karlovac, not Karlovčić); joined by a space ("Topolšica slove", "Sutjeska zeleng") it takes only an area the column itself writes after villages ("Topolšica, Slovenija", "Sutjeska, Zelengora"), one it writes rarely only near the village. A region of a land the village lies outside is refused (`in_land`, read from the GeoNames dumps our places tool caches, `data-extraction/.cache/map1941/geonames`, local), where GeoNames can tell: it lists every Slovene settlement (6,640 places against the register's 6,035) but misses many villages elsewhere (Klasnić by Glina, Pađani), so a Slovene region only after a village of the name in Slovenia and no other republic's region after one only in Slovenia. "Bosiljevo slove" stays as printed, Bosiljevo being in Croatia only (GeoNames 3203639; the card session's check of 2026-11-20), as do "Ludbreg sloveni", "Županja sloveni" and "Dravograd-slavo"; "Bosiljevo karlo" is Bosiljevo, Karlovac. Towns are not checked so: GeoNames knows their namesakes elsewhere, and striking a town out promoted a worse one. Where several stand close, the one that is a region or lies within 60 km of a village of the name wins ("Brodarevo Prije" Prijepolje, not Prijedor; "Krašić-jastreba" Jastrebarsko). Other rules: a printed caron must be kept ("gradač" is Gradačac, not Gradac); a whole word the site prints as a place stays unless the town it begins is twenty times commoner ("Negotino-vardar" stays; "Opatovac-vukova" is Vukovar); a cut preposition or common noun is no area ("Vinkov vrh Pri", "Cezanjevci mlin"); two letters are no area ("Gladno Polje-sa"). The places counted are the records' own, not those borrowed from the census nor those after a preposition ("kod Žabljaka"). Slovene small words go small ("Šmarje pri Jelšah"). Six completions read and found false are in `READ_AS_PRINTED` ("Patkovača belji": Bijeljina misspelt, the site's Beljin lies elsewhere). The dry run (`--cuts`) lists every completion with its count, its rivals and two census entries. The table took 728 rows that day (12,899 to 13,460): 187 whole names, 56 known only from the site, 440 areas after a space, and the hyphen-joined areas read again (about 100 more completed, 27 of the old guesses dropped: "Majevica-čatani" was Catania, "Vrtače.srem-fro" Frosinone). Emulated file by file (a full `apply_corrections.py` needs more than 3 GB, which the machine didn't have free), that changes the death place of 1,126 census-part records (once the 122 chain rebuilds them: a stored form joined with a comma, "Sv. Marija, Slove", is no key of the table, its raw value is) and of 41 unit records that borrowed the census's, and no other field; 32 of them take back one of the old table's guesses ("Mokro Polje, Knin" back to "Mokro Polje, Kn", "Romanija, Sokoca" to "Romanija, Sokoc"): read one by one most looked right (Ključ 8 km off, Knin 13, Zvornik 19, Marčana by Pula), about five wrong ("Babino Polje-ml" is Mljet, not Mladenovac 316 km off; "Lokve-gor,kotar" Gorski kotar, not Kotarani), the rest past telling, and the user chose on 2026-11-20 to keep all 32 as printed, the rule over hand-read rows. Only the cut values' rows were written: 21 other values that today's data reads otherwise ("Crna gora" Crna Gora on 1,939 records, "Železna kapla") were left as they were, and a later `--write` takes them along.
